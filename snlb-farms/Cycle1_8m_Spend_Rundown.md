# Cycle 1 — what the ₦8 million is spent on
## Ogun State, 1 hectare, Regular season (August 2026 prices)

**Entity:** SNLB ENTERPRISE (BN 6922259) trading as SNLB Farms  
**Pack:** ₦8,000,000 promoter equity — **₦3.0 million CapEx + ₦5.0 million Cycle 1 working capital**  
**Crop:** Hybrid tomato, drip + fertigation, 20 t/ha plan, harvest February–April, sold at Mile 12  
**Canonical copy:** this pack is printed in full in the circulating study, §6.11. Keep both files in step if either is edited.

This note does two jobs: (1) it uses a public Nigerian **1-hectare tomato business plan** as the structural model; (2) it retimes that model to **Ogun, 2026 money**, which is what ₦8 million actually buys today.

## 1. The model business plan (what to copy)

The cleanest public 1-ha tomato plan in Nigeria is **Veggie Concept / Veggie Grow**, *Tomato Farming: Business Plan, Cost, Revenue and Profit* (originally 2022; still the template those sites republish). It is a **skeleton**, not a budget you can plant on.

### Sections the model uses — and where they sit in SNLB

| Model plan section | What it covers | SNLB equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Farm set-up | Drip, soil/water test, staff training | §4.4–4.12 and this ₦8m pack |
| Business description | Plant → manage → harvest → sell | §2 operating model |
| Market analysis | Mile 12, April–August scarcity, agents 5–10% | §3; SNLB sells itself, cash T+0 |
| Competitive analysis | Most farmers skip drip and get 4–10 t/ha | §2.5–2.6 |
| Sales strategy | Open market vs retail/hotels | §4.5 Mile 12 SOP; bulk from Cycle 5 |
| Cost / revenue / profit | Three price scenarios | Planning ₦33k / stress ₦15k / caution ₦22k |
| Notes and assumptions | Yield, seed type, labour, drip | §4.8–4.9 and the model |

**Do not plant the 2022 naira in that template.** Their 1 ha opex was **₦720,000** (seed ₦120k, labour ₦240k, fertilizer ₦200k, drip CapEx ₦625k). At August 2026 shop prices those same items are several times higher. SNLB’s planning production cost is **₦3.4 million/ha Regular** plus CapEx, insurance, payroll and logistics — that is the intensive drip stand, not a 2022 backyard budget.

Other public references (structure only, not SNLB numbers):

- **dripirrigation.com.ng** — tomato drip steps: land prep → kit → water source → layout → install → test. Their 1 ha drip band **₦500,000–₦1,250,000** (laterals; not borehole + genset).
- **Farmsquare** — 1-acre vegetable drip kit **₦450,000–₦600,000** (≈ ₦1.1–₦1.5 million per hectare if you buy two-acre-equivalent laterals).
- **HTS Farms / FarmPays** — current hybrid seed and soluble-fertilizer tickets (cited below).
- **NIHORT** intensive range 20–40 t/ha — SNLB plans the **floor** (20 t Regular / 18 t Peak).

The circulating feasibility study is the full business plan for this farm. This file is the **Cycle 1 cash chapter**.

## 3. A. CapEx — ₦3,000,000 (the kit)

Priced for **Ogun** (generally cheaper drilling than Lagos rock; still get a meter-rate quote). A 9 kVA Mikano does **not** fit in this pack — Cycle 1 uses a small farm genset and a 2–4 m³/hour borehole.

| # | Item | 2026 shop band | Pack | Why this number |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| A1 | **Borehole + casing** (Ogun, farm depth) | ₦600,000–₦1,500,000 SW drill | 850,000 | Mid-low Ogun hole; quote by the metre |
| A2 | **Submersible pump, 2,000 L tank, fittings** | Pump ₦300,000–₦900,000 | 300,000 | Sized to ~55 m³/day on 1 ha drip |
| A3 | **1 ha drip + filter + fertigation tank + install** | ₦500,000–₦1,250,000/ha laterals; fertigation extra | 950,000 | Inside the model’s ₦1.0m/ha drip unit |
| A4 | **Small diesel/petrol genset (≈2.5–3.5 kVA)** | ~₦280,000–₦470,000 | 300,000 | Runs pump when grid is dead; not a 9 kVA Mikano |
| A5 | **Spare pump + drip-repair kit** | — | 50,000 | Irrigation failure is a yield event |
| A6 | **Plastic crates (40 units)** | ₦7,500–₦11,600 each | 320,000 | 40 × ₦8,000. Turn ~10× over a 5–6 week harvest (400 baskets) |
| A7 | **Nursery shade, trays, knapsack, hoes, trellis wire, posts** | — | 130,000 | Hardware that is not seed |
| A8 | **Land prep on 1 ha** (plough/harrow/ridge) | ₦80,000–₦150,000 | 100,000 | Tractor hire; Stage 0 is manual-plus-hire |
| | **CapEx total** | | **3,000,000** | |

**If the borehole quote comes in above ₦850,000:** cut crates to 30, or use a cheaper genset, or skip the tank stand. Do **not** drop drip quality. Yield is drip-dependent.

**If the land already has a working borehole:** A1–A2 (~₦1.15 million) can buy a better fertigation skid, more crates, or a solar-assist pump. Do not treat that as spare profit — keep it in CapEx.

## 6. What ₦8 million does **not** buy

- The other **99 hectares** of drip, a second borehole, packing shed, packhouse, or a 9 kVA genset.
- A FarmPark **co-investment** or profit share (not in this pack, and not allowed).
- Cycle 2’s extra **2 ha drip (₦2.0 million)** — that is Cycle 1 retained profit, after the go/no-go.
- Holding-fee rent on unused land (option/ROFR instead).
- A paste plant, cold chain, or a second 100 ha.

## 7. 2022 model vs 2026 Ogun pack (why the old plan understates)

| Item (1 ha) | Veggie Concept 2022 | SNLB pack 2026 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Drip CapEx | 625,000 | 1,000,000 laterals + 1,250,000 water/power around it |
| Hybrid seed | 120,000 | 1,020,000 (seed + nursery) |
| Fertilizer | 200,000 | 680,000 (and shop tickets can print ~₦770k) |
| Pesticides | 70,000 | 408,000 |
| Labour (6 months) | 240,000 | 612,000 field + 420,000 manager |
| Farm rent | 25,000 | 100,000 cultivated |
| Tools / sprayer | 15,000 | inside ₦280k hardware |
| **Cash to plant one intensive hectare** | **~₦1.3m including drip** | **₦8.0m** |

The model plan’s **shape** is right. Its **naira is not**. Planting on ₦720k opex in Ogun in 2026 is a Roma-and-rain farm, not a 20 t/ha drip farm.
