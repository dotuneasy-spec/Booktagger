## 3. Gaps you must fill (nothing public closes these)

Tick these in the data room. Until they are closed, FarmPark is a **named intention**, not a bankable FMC.

### A. Legal — counsel, not LinkedIn

- [ ] CAC search: exact name, **RC number**, status (active), directors, shareholders, charges.
- [ ] Confirm whether **FarmPark Limited** exists separately from **FarmAgro Projects Limited**, and which entity signs the advisory letter and later SLA.
- [ ] Authorised signatories; board resolution to contract with SNLB.
- [ ] Related-party map: Revelation Properties, land SPVs, input companies, any entity that would sell seed, drip or diesel to SNLB.
- [ ] TIN, VAT (if applicable), registered address matching King’s Deck.

### B. Contract type — decide this in meeting one

- [ ] Written confirmation they will take **advisory terms for Cycles 1–2** (Stage 0), not a co-investment.
- [ ] They will **not** take a slice of NPAT (that would break 50/50).
- [ ] They will **not** require SNLB to buy FarmPark land or to put SNLB hectares into a FarmPark investor pool.
- [ ] Later SLA (from Cycle 3/4) is a **services contract** with naira fees, mobilisation KPIs, and termination for missed yield / missed Peak window.

### C. Tomato, drip, Ogun — proof or walk

Ask for **three named reference farms**, on paper, with:

- [ ] Crop (tomato or not), cultivar, open-field vs poultry/fish.
- [ ] Hectares actually managed (not “acres in the brochure”).
- [ ] Yield (t/ha) for the last two cycles, not an 85% “return.”
- [ ] Whether they sold into **Mile 12**, and at what realised ₦/basket.
- [ ] Drip / fertigation SOP they would use on SNLB’s 1 ha.
- [ ] Permission to **visit one working farm** before you pay a retainer.

If they cannot show a tomato drip cycle, they can still be a **land/admin advisor**, but you then hire a separate agronomist / farm manager for Cycle 1. Do not pretend they are the tomato operator.

### D. Fees and procurement — naira, on letterhead

- [ ] Cycle 1–2 advisory retainer (₦), what it covers, what it does not.
- [ ] Whether they mark up seed, drip, fertilizer, or diesel (related-party margin).
- [ ] Who holds supplier contracts: SNLB or FarmPark.
- [ ] Mechanization rental rates at 5 / 25 / 60 ha, against the study’s 7 / 14 / 20% production-cost discounts.
- [ ] Payment terms: never pay a year of fees up front; cycle-in-arrears against the KPI pack.

### E. People on the Ogun site

- [ ] **Named farm manager** who will live on or weekly-run the Ogun hectare (CV: tomato or intensive vegetable, drip, labour gangs).
- [ ] Confirm they accept the study KPI pack: Cycle 1 yield ≥ 18 t/ha (plan 20); spoilage <5%; Regular harvest **February–April**; Peak window July–November.
- [ ] Organogram: who in Lekki vs who in Ogun; labour PAYE / pension — their payroll or SNLB’s.
- [ ] How they staff +15 / +27 / +45 ha in one six-month window (written, with subcontractors named).

### F. Water, power, insurance, HSE

- [ ] Borehole / pumping note that can scale to 100 ha **before Cycle 5**, not a 1 ha kit only.
- [ ] Genset / spare-pump practice matching the study’s Cycle 1 power pack.
- [ ] NAIC interface and GIT: who binds, who is named insured.
- [ ] HSE and chemical handling SOP; PPE; incident reporting.

### G. Conflicts and land

- [ ] Written non-circumvention: they do not shop SNLB’s optioned 100 ha to their co-investors.
- [ ] They do not earn a land-sale commission on this block.
- [ ] Any Ogun land they already manage is disclosed (adjacent competition or water conflict).

### H. Capacity versus the 100 ha path

- [ ] Written confirmation they can support the path **1 → 3 → 15 → 27 → 72 → 100 ha**.
- [ ] If they cannot mobilise +45 ha, the study already says: **cut the ceiling** and slip the add to the next Peak — get that in the SLA, not as a surprise in Cycle 5.

## 4. What to ask in the first meeting (take this list)

Meet **David Omaghomi** at King’s Deck. Do not send the lease, the ₦8 million proof of funds, or the full model in the first sitting. Send the one-page ask below.

1. CAC certificate, status report, and the name of the contracting company.
2. Organogram and the CV of the person who would run SNLB’s Ogun hectare.
3. Three reference farms (crop, ha, yield, buyer). Offer a site visit.
4. Draft **advisory letter for Cycle 1 only** (Regular, 1 ha, February–April harvest, drip, Mile 12).
5. Fee quote in naira: advisory retainer; later SLA; any input margin.
6. Confirmation in writing: no co-investment, no FarmPark land, no NPAT share.
7. Confirmation they can staff **Ogun**, not only Lekki / Ibadan fish–poultry.
8. Related-party disclosure (Revelation Properties and any input company).

**Walk-away tests (any one is enough):** they insist on co-investment or a profit share; they cannot name a tomato or intensive-vegetable farm you can visit; they will only contract as “FarmPark” with no CAC entity; they want to replace SNLB’s 100 ha option with their own land product.

## 7. Bottom line

FarmPark is a **real Lekki office** (King’s Deck, Chevron) that has been trading a farm-investment and “we manage, you earn” brand since about 2017–18. Public material does **not** yet show a tomato drip operator who can take SNLB from 1 ha to 100 ha on the circulating study’s terms.

That is not a reason to drop them. It is a reason to **treat the first meeting as due diligence**, get the CAC entity and a Cycle 1 advisory letter, visit a working farm, and keep 50/50 and the Ogun option in SNLB’s name. Close the ticks in section 3 before you pay a retainer or name them as operator in a credit file.
