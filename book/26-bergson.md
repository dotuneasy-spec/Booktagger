# Bergson in a School Uniform

Henri Bergson did not write about Class D. He wrote about time, intellect, intuition, and a living mix that does not behave like a row of beads. This book has used him because Ayanokōji’s problem and Bergson’s problem rhyme: the mind that only cuts, versus the mind that can coincide.

### Duration, said again, slower

Clock time is a line of equal units. Lived time is not. A minute of humiliation is not the same size as a minute of a boring class, even if the clock says it is. In duration, states interpenetrate. A present that seems only present is thick with past (memory pressing) and with a kind of future (tendency, aim, fear).

Students live this and then deny it with schedules. He uses it. He knows that a person’s “now” is a contraction of a thickness. If you only answer the contraction, you miss the thickness. Mapping is, in this light, a way of guessing what is pressing from behind the now. Intuition is a way of feeling the thickness without a list.

His trained memory is a mechanical helper of duration: more past can press because more past is available. Bergson would not call a White Room memory true intuition. True intuition is coincidence, not storage. Storage is intellect’s cousin. Still, storage changes the feel of the now. He is never only in the minute. That is agency and exile together.

### Qualitative multiplicity

A feeling is not one thing plus another thing. Anger-with-shame-with-a-wish-to-laugh is one mix, like a chord, not like three coins. Nodes are where a mix has been pulled into a repeatable chord. He hears chords in people. He names them poorly on purpose (he does not give lectures). He acts as if the chord will play again under similar weather. Often it will. When it will not, the person has differed. He updates.

His own mix is unusually simple in tone: curiosity, mild irritation, throttle, a thin wish, a hard refusal. Simple is not the same as empty. A single note can be loud. His note is quiet and continuous. Continuous quiet is the phenomenology of one being in instrumental tone.

### Intellect’s legitimate job

Bergson is sometimes misread as anti-intellect. He is not. Intellect is how you make tools, plans, cuts, exams, knives, class ranks. The school is an intellect-machine. He is a virtuoso of that machine. The warning is only: intellect spatializes. It turns a living motion into parts on a table. If you then believe the table is the animal, you will be surprised when the animal moves.

He cuts, then returns to the motion (a talk, a look, a week), then cuts again. That oscillation is why he is better than a pure schemer on paper. Pure paper misses tone. Tone is qualitative. Tone is why a plan that “should work” fails in a room: the chord was wrong.

### Élan, and why he is not a vitalist hero

Bergson’s *élan vital* is a creative push, life differentiating into forms. Readers who want Ayanokōji to be a life-genius sometimes paste this onto him. The paste does not hold. His animation is real, but the tone is not overflowing creation. It is conservation, ranking, use. A vitalist hero would waste, bloom, risk form for more life. He hates waste. He is closer to a craftsman of scarce motion. Scarce motion can still be singular. It is not Bergson’s sunniest myth.

The poetic why is closer to Bergson than the workshop is. Why-intuition coincides with a motion to know its quality, not to spend it. He spends. Bergson, at his most useful for this book, is the name of the floor the boy underuses.

### Hesitation

Bergson connected freedom to a widened zone where the living creature is not already a reflex. Hesitation can be the space of choice.

He is paradoxical here. He shortens his own hesitation (tool). He lengthens other people’s (medium). In them, hesitation is when a new sentence can be born. In him, hesitation is often already resolved by rank. Rank is a kind of freedom (the now does not own him) and a kind of unfreedom (the rank was trained). Bergsonian freedom would be a self that coincides with its whole duration and acts from that whole, not from a drilled setting. He acts from a drilled whole. That is not nothing. It is not the philosopher’s freedom either.

### Sympathy

Intuition as sympathy: entering, not measuring. His talks have a cold sympathy — timed to their clock, not melted into their feeling. Melted would be specific, or sentimental, or both. Timed is enough to be uncanny. Uncanny is the word students would use if they had it: he gets it, and he is not with me.

Bergson wanted sympathy as a way of knowing life. He uses a stripped version as a way of knowing opponents and allies. Same faculty, thinner love.
