# Objections

## Answers to Likely Complaints

### “You made him a philosopher. He is just a tactician.”

He does not need to read Bergson to be describable in Bergson’s words. A craftsman does not need the physics of tension to use a lever. This book is a description from outside and from a conversation that already used those words. The image of thought can be present without the syllabus.

### “You moralized a light novel.”

The source conversation included methods for moving people. A book that used those methods as a celebration would be a manual with a character sticker on it. Fiction is a decent place to study empty knives. Morality is the handle. If you want only cool, the novels are already there.

### “Singular/specific is academic noise.”

Try living without the distinction. You will still need a way to say: one inner engine versus many real others. Those are the two poles of his life. The words are shorter than the paragraphs they save.

### “He does change; you froze him.”

The book says the core is singular and can widen. Widening is not denied. Conversion to specific love is not assumed. Serialization may do what it wants. A study of an image of thought is allowed to describe a stable geometry while a plot moves furniture.

### “The poetic layer is you, not him.”

Yes and no. He underuses it. The conversation asked for it. A study of a mind includes the floor the mind barely walks, the way a study of a house includes the locked room. The locked room is part of the house.

### “This is too long.”

The request was a long book and long chapters. Repetition is walking around a joint. Skip with the contents page. The spine chapter is a short path.

### “Can I use this to be more like him?”

You can occupy thinking as one being, rank a trivial now, make-do with incomplete marks, and stay for a why. You should not treat people as terrain. If a sentence in this book helps you map *yourself*, good. If it helps you hunt, you have flipped the knife and this afterword will not save you. Put it down.

### “Where are the quotes from the novels?”

Omitted on purpose. This is original analysis, not a reprint. Paraphrase of public character facts is enough for a diagram. If you want his voice, read the novels. If you want the joints named, read this.
