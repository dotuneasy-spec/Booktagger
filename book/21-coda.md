# Coda

## What the Tool-Self Cannot Do

A long book about a powerful mind can start to sound like praise. This coda is the counterweight.

Ayanokōji can do almost everything a contest requires. He can see past the loud now, map a room, wait, speak little, strike once, let others own the winning sentence, and remain, as far as possible, unnamed. That is a completed form of singular animation in a hostile medium. It is also a form with holes that competence cannot patch.

### It cannot meet another person as specific

He can model them. Modeling is still a function of one identity. The specific — the stubborn fact of another life that does not exist as his fold — is available to him as information and rarely as relation. When relation happens anyway, he experiences it as noise, exception, or new equipment. The person on the other side may experience love, use, rescue, or a confusing mix. He does not have a native grammar for “you are not for anything, including me.”

Without that grammar, even his care is shaped like a tool. Care-shaped-like-a-tool can save you and still leave a bruise that has no name.

### It cannot let some nows be sovereign

Optional contraction was the healthy goal. He overshot into optional presence. A joke that is only a joke, a kindness that is only kindness, a harm that should stop the plan — these require a surrender of ranking. He ranks. Ranking is his loyalty to the one being as workshop. Some loyalties are too loyal.

### It cannot generate a why that is not a use

The poetic layer knocks. He files. Meaning remains operational: what is this for? A life asked that way will always answer with a task. Tasks end. When the last task ended, the emptiness would still be there, now unemployed. Freedom-from is his masterpiece. Freedom-for is unwritten.

### It cannot be scattered, which means it cannot play

Play, real play, needs a kind of wasting, a kind of being-many, a kind of now that does not report to a ministry of aims. He can imitate play. Imitation is a mask. Children in ordinary rooms learn to be stupid on purpose, together. He learned that stupidity is a leak. So he is never quite in the game he is playing. The school is full of games. He is a referee disguised as a player, or a player disguised as furniture. Neither is play.

### It cannot confess without a strategy of confession

Even honesty, in him, tends to be a move. There may be true sentences. Their timing is still a craft. A mind that cannot be uncrafted cannot be received, only interpreted. That is lonely in a way that company does not fix. Company interprets. Reception requires a moment when the one being is not producing an effect.

### It cannot, by itself, become good

This is the hard sentence.

Competence is not goodness. Mapping is not respect. Conversation-as-medium is how teachers heal and how con men hunt. The structure this book described is morally empty in the way a knife is empty. He sometimes uses the knife to cut a rope off someone. He sometimes uses it to cut a person into a useful shape. The singular tool-self does not contain a law that decides which. Any law would have to come from the specific — from others as others, from a why that is not his project.

Readers who love him for winning are entitled to the entertainment. Readers who want to live should not confuse his geometry with a virtue.

### What remains worth learning, named without romance

A few capacities are real and portable, and they do not require treating people as terrain:

The present is a contraction, not the world.

Thinking can be occupied as one being rather than as a committee of panics.

Deeper causation is often implied by a surface that works too hard.

You can start from a chosen aim without announcing it as a sermon, in your own life, about your own habits.

Intuition has a how-floor and a why-floor. Do not skip the second.

Making-do with incomplete marks is a way of learning. It is not a way of owning.

Flights that return are healthier than flights that hover.

If you take only these, you have not become Ayanokōji. You have used a fictional extreme as a diagram of mind. That is the only honest use of him this book will endorse.

### Last picture

A boy sits in a class that thinks he is nobody.

He is not nobody. He is one being, self-moving, trained as a tool, superb at how, poor at why, able to tell more than the moment tells him, unable to let the moment tell him enough.

The students around him are scattered, loud, specific, sometimes stupid, sometimes kinder than he can be because they do not rank every kindness.

The book has been the boy. The world is the room. A whole mind would have to be both: singular enough to have agency, specific enough to have company, poetic enough to know what either is for.

He is the first. The rest is the work he cannot do for you, and cannot quite do for himself.
