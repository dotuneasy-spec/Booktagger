# Language

## How a Dry Mind Thinks in Words

His narration, as the novels style it, is dry, exact, sometimes mildly comic, rarely lyrical. Style is not costume only. It is the image of thought in grammar.

### Short closures

He likes sentences that close. Closure is a conclusive formulation applied to himself. The world happened; here is the file. Lyric prefers sentences that stay open. Open sentences are why-floor. He writes (thinks) in files.

### Few metaphors

Metaphor is a way of coinciding with tone. He can understand metaphor as a tool in talk (a parallel for someone else). He does not live in metaphor. Living in metaphor would be poetic mechanism. His inner speech is closer to protocol and observation. Protocol is how.

### Humor as throttle and camouflage

Dry humor lowers the rank of a now (it cannot be a tragedy if it can be a slight joke) and signals harmlessness. It is also a remainder: comedy is often unspendable. A joke that is only a joke is ordinary. He is capable of those. They may be his most ordinary minutes.

### Silence

Silence is a large part of his language. In conversation, silence is a rest for the other line. Inwardly, silence is the one being without a committee. People who fear silence fill it. He does not fear it. The Room was silent in a white way. School silence is different: full of other people’s noise at the edge. He can sit in the difference. Sitting there is almost dwelling. Then a therefore arrives.

### What he cannot say

I need you (without a structure).
This day is enough.
I am hurt (without a file).
I do not know (without a placement).

The unsayable is the outline of the locked room. A book about his mind should include the words that do not occur. Absence is data in a study, not in his workshop.
