# Winning

## The Only Criterion, and Its Hole

Victory is the public name of his animation’s direction. The White Room scored. The school scores. He kept the habit of a score even when he refused the scorer.

### What winning means, operationally

Not always first place. Often: the field after the event is acceptable to the origin-sentence. He can “lose” a visible game and win the cycle (still unnamed, class still viable, rival overextended). Visible loss as actual win is literacy in climate. Students who only know surface-winning hate this. They think he is throwing. He is ranking.

### Winning as absorption

A win is when leftovers get filed. The week closes. The sentence holds. Unfiled remainders feel like not-yet-winning, even if the points went up. That is why a personal remainder can bother him more than a lost quiz. The quiz was climate. The remainder is a leak in the one field.

### The hole

If winning is the only criterion, the criterion cannot ask what winning is for. How cannot grade why. He can win a hallway forever. Fans call the forever “based.” This book calls it a hole in the metric. A metric with a hole will optimize for more hallway.

A different criterion — was there a remainder that was honored, was a now enough, was a person not folded — would look like losing in his native language. That is the cost of a single score. Single scores are singular. Specific life needs more than one.

### Winning and ordinary

Ordinary life, if he got it, would refuse to be won. You do not win a park. You inhabit it. Inhabiting is a different verb from operating. The wish for ordinary is a wish to retire the only criterion. He has not retired it. He has used ordinary as a tactic of hiding, which is winning by another name.

Until winning is demoted from god to tool, the tool-self cannot stop being a tool. Demotion would be a dialectic: thesis, I win; antithesis, some goods are not victories; synthesis, I can win when winning is the right verb, and not otherwise. He has the first. The second knocks. The third is the unfinished book inside this book.
