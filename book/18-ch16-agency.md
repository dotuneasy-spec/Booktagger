# Chapter 16

## Present Context and Agency

The problem was stated without costume: *I can only see as far as present context as it comes up. I want to separate myself from that and be able to tell more by volition and agency.*

Ayanokōji is a portrait of someone who solved the first half hard, and maybe too hard.

### What present context does

The now is a contraction. It pulls memory, fear, hope, and the body’s alarms into a tight beam. The beam is useful. It is how you catch a ball and how you answer a question and how you step off a road.

The beam becomes a prison when it claims to be the whole field. Then every message is the day. Every face is a verdict. Every feeling is a law. You cannot “tell more” because there is no more — only the next thing that arises, and you.

Bergson described the intellect as turning toward practical action, tightening around what must be done. The reversal — intuition — turns back into the flow of duration, where the now is a bright point in a moving thickness. Agency, in this vocabulary, is the capacity to perform that reversal on purpose, not only when exhaustion does it to you.

### His solution

He was trained until the now is almost never the boss. The now is a mark in a longer cycle. He chooses the cycle. That is volition as he knows it.

He does not, typically, fight the now with willpower theatrics. Theatrics would still be the now, only louder. He occupies a prior animation and lets the now take a smaller rank. Insult arrives; rank of insult is low; rank of the month’s aim is high; action follows rank, not volume.

This feels like coldness. It is ranking.

### Separation without numbness

The request was to *separate* from present context, not to go dead.

Numbness is a fake separation. The now still rules; you just stopped feeling the whip. His blank face can look like numbness. Under it, ranking is active. Active ranking is alive. Numbness is a wall.

A usable separation has three parts, which this book will not dress up as a White Room drill, only as a description of a capacity:

You notice that a contraction is happening. (“This argument is filling the whole screen of mind.”)

You remember, or feel, that a wider field exists. (The week, the aim, the one being of thinking, the fact that this tone has happened before and ended.)

You choose which cycle to prescribe a solution for, in Uexküll’s sense. (Is the solution “win this minute,” or “keep the longer melody”?)

He does this in milliseconds. A person who does not would do it in seconds, then in a minute, then as a habit. The form is the same.

### Future-back and past-press

Two cheap, real ways the field gets wider:

Let the intended later state give the now a job. Not fantasy. Rank. If in some months you want to be a person who is not owned by every ping, then this ping is a rehearsal, not a god.

Let the past press, on purpose. Not rumination. Rumination is the now eating the past. Press is: this pattern is old; I have been this contraction before; I am also the one who has left it before.

His memory makes the second automatic. His aims make the first automatic. You would have to borrow both by hand at first.

### Agency is not total prediction

People think agency means seeing the future. He is good at likely futures. That is not the core.

The core is: **the meaning of the now is not dictated by the now.** He assigns meaning from the animation. That is “telling more by volition.” Telling, here, is not narration to an audience. It is the inner act of saying what this is.

If someone else assigns the meaning — the school, a parent, a mood, a crowd — you have perception without agency. You still see. You see only as far as they shine a light.

### The cost, again, because this is the chapter where it matters

If you win the war against present context too completely, you cannot be found by a moment that *should* be the boss: a person in front of you, a beauty, a harm you are doing, a joke, a death. Some nows deserve sovereignty.

He is bad at granting that sovereignty. That is why he can be morally late. The harm was a mark in a cycle. The person was a now that should have interrupted the cycle. Interruption would have been agency of another kind: the agency to be stopped.

So the goal is not “become him.” The goal is **optional contraction**. Tighten when a ball is in the air. Widen when the mind has been captured by a trivial tyrant. Let a real now through.

He has optional tightening. He has less optional widening into presence. You want both.

### ### Volition is ranking, not grinding

People try to get agency by clenching. Clenching is still the now, plus muscle. He does not clench much. He assigns rank. A thing of rank zero can be left unanswered without a heroic struggle. The struggle was a sign that the thing still had unauthorized rank.

If you cannot yet rank, you can delay. Delay is a crude cousin of ranking: the now does not get the first vote. He uses delay on others as a tool. Used on yourself, delay is a way of letting the one being catch up to the contraction. Ten minutes is arbitrary and sometimes enough. The point is the gap, not the number.

### Telling more

“Tell more by volition” means: produce more of the field than the stimulus contained. Implication, memory, aim, self-differing. The now gave you a sentence. You can answer from the sentence, or from the month, or from the one being. He answers from a place the interlocutor cannot see, which is why his replies can seem too small or too sideways. They are sized to a cycle you are not in.

The non-rotten version: when asked a question, notice which cycle you are about to answer from. Choose. That is already more agency than most rooms get from you.

A picture

A notification. Heart rate. Story: I must answer or I will lose something.

Prison: answer, or suffer the not-answering as the whole evening.

His way: the notification is a mark. The cycle might be “this person,” or “this work,” or “my hiding,” or “nothing.” He picks. Sometimes he picks nothing, which looks like calm and is a ranking of zero.

Another way, which he underuses: the notification is a person. The cycle is their duration meeting yours. The solution is not a tactic. It is a presence. That is floor-two intuition applied to a phone, which sounds comic and is most of modern life.
