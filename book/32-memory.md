# Memory

## The Past That Presses on Purpose

Ayanokōji’s memory is part of the legend: near-perfect, trained, unfair. This chapter is not a hymn to a superpower. It is about what memory *does* in a singular field.

### Bergson’s press, mechanized

For Bergson, the past is not a box of files behind us. It presses into the present as a virtual thickness. Most people feel this as a smell that opens a year, or a humiliation that arrives in a new room uninvited.

His training made the press selectable. He can let a relevant past arrive as working material. He can, to a degree, keep an irrelevant past from becoming a mood-government. Selectable press is a how-floor miracle. It is also how he avoids being owned by memory-as-trauma in the ordinary teenage way. The Room still presses. He ranks it. Ranking a trauma is not healing. It is operationalizing. Operationalized trauma is still in the medium. It will shape what self-differing can produce: more threat-intelligence, more throttle, more refusal to be owned.

### Memory as map-thickness

A thin sample becomes a type when it sits beside other samples. Memory is how implication works at speed. Today’s laugh-too-hard is not a first laugh. The type “status payment” lights up. He does not always need a new theory. He needs a match.

This is why he can look psychic in a first meeting. It is not the first meeting in his medium. It is the first meeting in their calendar.

The ethical risk: types erase remainders. A person who is almost a type but not quite gets handled as the type. That is intellect’s spatializing, applied to a life. He is usually right enough to win. Right enough is not true enough.

### Forgetting as a skill he may lack

Ordinary minds forget, and forgetting is sometimes mercy — for them and for others. He keeps. Keeping is a contest advantage. Keeping is also how a workshop never gets a new room: the old tools are all still on the wall, including the ones that should have been thrown out. A poetic why might require a kind of forgetting: let a day not become data. He is poorly built for that mercy.

### Memory and one being

If thinking is one being, memory is not a warehouse the being visits. Memory is the being’s thickness. He is thick with tasks, thin with useless days. Thickness of tasks makes him deep in one direction. Ask him to be thick with summers, jokes, a parent’s ordinary voice, and the medium has gaps. Gaps are why ordinary life feels foreign. He cannot remember how to do it because there is almost nothing in the thickness to press.

You cannot self-differ into a past you do not have. You can only sample a present and wait for it to become past. That is why his wish for ordinary is slow. He is trying to grow a thickness the Room never planted. Growing it would mean letting nows be sovereign long enough to become memory that is not data. He rarely lets them.
