# The Father

## Manufacturer, Enemy, and Inner Weather

Atsuomi Ayanokōji (as the story gives him) is not only a villain in a plot. He is the first animator who tried to own the animation.

### Product versus self-positing

A manufacturer says: I am the origin; you are the result. A singular tool-self says: I act. The whole character is a fight between those two sentences. Competence is what the manufacturer wanted. Stolen authorship is what the boy took. The school is a place to practice authorship under a fake name.

If you forget the father, the tool-self looks like a fashion of coldness. With the father, coldness is a lock on a stolen engine. Warmth looked, in that house of a facility, like a way to be scored and used. So warmth is still suspicious. Suspicion is inner weather, not an argument.

### The father as a specific other who demanded to be a God

Here the singular/specific distinction turns. The father refused to be specific — a limited man in relation to a child. He tried to be the One of the child’s world. The child became a One in self-defense. Two singularities, one household, no we. The we was a laboratory.

This is why “just love him and he’ll thaw” is a fan-sentence that may fail. Thawing would mean allowing a specific claim into the origin. The first specific claim he knew was predatory. The medium learned: claims are how products are recalled.

### Inner father-weather

Even away, the manufacturer presses as virtual past. A camera in the sky of the mind. Ranking, invisibility, refusal of thrones — some of this is freedom-from that man. If the man vanished, some of the workshop might have no enemy to justify it. Then the why-floor would be unavoidable: if not against him, then for what. That might be the most frightening exam, because no one is administering it.

### Not becoming the father

The live moral risk is mimesis. A boy who was used becomes someone who uses, with better stealth. The structure rhymes. He can tell himself the difference: I do not manufacture children; I navigate a school; I want out. Differences matter. Rhyme matters too. Mapping people as terrain is a descendant of a lab that mapped children as data. This book will not scream the rhyme on every page. It will not pretend the rhyme is absent.

If he has a chance at a different animation, it is curiosity and the remainder — the parts of the medium the father did not plant. Those parts are small. They are the only seeds of a for.
