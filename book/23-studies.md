# Studies

## Other Minds as Mirrors

A singular mind is easiest to see against other minds it cannot fully absorb. This section is not a character guide. It is a set of mirrors. Each person around Ayanokōji reveals a limit or a talent of the tool-self by being a different image of thought.

### Horikita: the blade that wants a name

Horikita Suzune wants worth to be visible, earned, and clean. Effort, leadership, the brother’s shadow, the refusal of cheap warmth — these are not decorations. They are her animation’s tone: merit as identity.

He does not share that religion. Worth, for him, is a school-surface. He can still *use* the religion because it is a strong motor. Class D needs a public protagonist. She needs a war that looks like merit. The counterpoint writes itself: her line is pride-as-worth; his line is viability-without-a-name. He lets her melody stay recognizable. If he turned her into a meek assistant, the melody would die and the class would lose its joint.

What he cannot do is pray with her. He can respect the blade as a tool. She wants the blade to be a soul. When she grows, it is often specific growth — becoming a person among persons, a leader who is not only a wound. His growth, if any, is still singular: a wider workshop. They can walk the same corridor and not be in the same metaphysics.

The tool-self likes her because she is predictable in the honorable sense: she will try. Predictable honor is rare. He will not mock it except mildly. Mockery would dull the blade. He needs the edge.

The remainder: she wants to be seen by someone who is not using the seeing. He sees with a job. She may get the most competent ally in the building and still be lonely in the ally. That loneliness is the specific knocking on the singular.

### Ryūen: heat singularity

Ryūen Kakeru is also one field. The field is domination. Witnesses are required. Pain is a language. He absorbs people the way a fire absorbs air.

Put two singularities in a room and you do not get a democracy. You get a contest of animations. Ryūen’s tone is theatrical. Ayanokōji’s is pale. Theater needs a stage. Pale needs none. So the pale one can let the theater run until the theater meets a fact it cannot bully: there is always a quieter teeth.

He does not hate Ryūen in a moral way. Hate would be a contraction. He files him as a climate event that can be steered. Sometimes he needs the storm. Sometimes he needs the storm to break itself. Ryūen, for his part, is one of the few who can smell that the average boy is not a backing track. Smell is already a kind of map.

The mirror: Ayanokōji without throttle would be closer to this heat — a tool-self that enjoys the display of control. He throttles. Throttle is not kindness. It is the refusal of a face. Ryūen wants a face, a legend, a fear in other people’s mouths. That want is a node. It is also a recapture waiting to happen. The pale singularity studies the hot one as a warning: visibility is a leash.

### Ichinose: the we that almost animates

Ichinose Honami is the closest the early story gets to a specific animation that works. People as ends, a class as a circle, trust as method. It is easy to call her naive. Naivety is the wall critics see. Under it is a real motor: a we that produces motion without a hidden king.

He can model her bubble. Marks, for her, include other people’s feelings as facts that obligate. Prescribed solutions: include, forgive, carry. The cycle closes when the group is intact. His cycle closes when the field is viable and he is unnamed. Intact and viable are not always the same. Intact can lose an exam. Viable can sacrifice a person.

He is cautious with her because breaking that motor might be expensive in a way that is not only moral. A class without any we is only a pile of nodes. Piles are hard to steer without becoming a tyrant, and tyrants are visible. So her kindness is useful to him as climate. Using kindness as climate is already a blasphemy in her religion. He will commit the blasphemy softly.

The mirror: if he ever immigrated into another Umwelt, hers would be the hardest and the most healing — marks that are not contest-marks. He visits. He does not move in. Moving in would rewrite the origin. The origin is still stolen property.

### Sakayanagi: the reader

Arisu Sakayanagi is dangerous because she is not only a rival in strength. She is a rival in *reading*. She is interested in the author-problem, the product-problem, the boy as a demonstration who refuses to demonstrate. That interest is already on his depth-layer.

Two mappers mapping each other is a different game from a mapper among the unmapped. Flattery, cruelty, games-as-conversation — she can play on a board he cannot dismiss as noise. He can still hide. Hiding from a reader is more expensive than hiding from a crowd. The crowd looks at surfaces. She looks for causation.

The mirror: he is not the only singular intellect in the building. His uniqueness was a White Room myth. The world contains other workshops. Some have different tones — play, aristocracy, boredom, delight in other people’s puzzles. If his tone is the workshop at night, hers is often the game in a garden. Games still absorb people. The absorption has a smile. He should recognize the smile. Whether he grants her the dignity of being more than a boss-fight is a test of whether his mapping includes remainders.

### Karuizawa: the remainder that will not file

Karuizawa Kei is where the diagram smudges.

A pawn-structure is consistent with the tool-self: a partner as camouflage, as information, as a node of status and fear that can be stabilized. The story keeps putting extra weight on the structure. Extra weight is the remainder. A person who should have been a function keeps being a person.

He can explain the relationship in workshop language for a long time. Explanation is how. The why knocks when a day with her is not only a day of maintaining a structure. If he feels that and files it, the tool-self has won another round. If he feels that and cannot file, the dialectic of the core has begun in earnest: I operate; something is not for operating.

This book will not pretend to know the end of a serialized novel. It will say: this is the site where singular absorption is most visibly strained. Readers who want him to “finally love” are asking for a metaphysical conversion, not only a romance subplot. Love in the strong sense would be specific — she is not a fold. He can approximate care as maintenance. Maintenance is not the conversion.

The mirror: his own ownership-node meets her belonging-node. He will not be owned. She has lived the terror of being left and used. A craftsman can stabilize her. A craftsman can also become a gentler owner. Whether he becomes a companion instead is the poetic layer applied to a pair of students. The workshop does not have a part called companion. That is why the relationship feels like a glitch in the machine, and why it is the most important glitch to watch.

### Class D as a many-body problem

The class is specific reality: too many bodies, stupid timing, wasted motion, sudden loyalty, sudden rot. He does not love the waste. He uses the class as a medium anyway, because the school made the class a unit of fate.

A many-body problem cannot be solved by mapping one genius. It is solved, when it is, by myths, joints, a few motors, and luck. He provides weather. They provide labor. Commentators who give him every victory are telling a singular story about a specific pile. The pile did the sweating. His mind is the one this book studies. The sweat still happened in other bodies. The coda’s demand — that others are not folds — starts here, in a classroom that is not a brain.
