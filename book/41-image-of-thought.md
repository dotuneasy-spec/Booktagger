# The Image of Thought

## What This Phrase Was For

“Image of thought” is a philosopher’s phrase (Deleuze used it for the unspoken presuppositions of thinking). This book stole it for a character: not what he thinks about, but what thinking *is* for him, before any content.

### His image, compressed

Thinking is one being.
It animates itself.
The now is a contraction, not a king.
Other people appear as maps, joints, lines in a counterpoint.
Truth is not declared; it is housed in their structures or not at all.
Problems yield sentences.
Depth is implied by surfaces that work too hard.
Intuition is exact coincidence, mostly used as how.
Flights are immanent.
The medium yields new information if you occupy it.
A face is a recapture.
Winning is the native criterion.
Why is a locked room.

That list is the book. The chapters were the walking.

### Other images, briefly

A sentimental image: thinking is feeling fully.
A scholastic image: thinking is arguing well.
A market image: thinking is optimizing.
A civic image: thinking is deliberating among equals.

He is closest to the market image with a singular metaphysics under it. Optimizing plus one being plus stolen origin. That is why he is not just a gifted student. Gifted students can still be committees. He is a field.

### Why the image matters more than feats

Feats date. A volume’s twist dates. The image is why he is still interesting after you know he is strong. Strength is a surface. The image is causation. Readers who only want feats will find this book slow. It was written for the other reader: the one who asked about multiplicity, animation, intuition, one being, and a poetic why.

### Changing an image of thought

Harder than changing an opinion. An opinion is content. An image is how content can appear. He can change opinions (tactics) without changing the image (singular workshop). People who wait for him to “open his heart” are waiting for an image-change. Those are rare, slow, and look like a different life, not a different speech.

If you, reading, want less capture by the now, you are asking for a small image-shift: from “the present is the world” to “the present is a contraction of one being.” That shift is available without his crimes or his childhood. The rest of his image — people as terrain, winning as god — is not required and not recommended.

This book was an attempt to make his image thinkable enough that you can take the first shift and leave the rest on the page, where it cannot touch anyone who did not ask.
