# Remainders

## The Things That Will Not Become Tools

A remainder is whatever survives the workshop’s digestion: a person after their function, a feeling after its file, a day after its move, a tone after its therefore.

This book has used the word as a hinge. Here it is gathered.

### Remainder as the specific

If absorption were perfect, there would be no remainder. Other people would close like objects in a functional cycle. They do not. They keep meaning something. That leftover meaning is the specific leaking into a singular field. He can call the leak noise. Noise is a way not to hear a world.

Kei as remainder has been named. There are smaller ones: a classmate’s stupid joke that is not a wall; a view of water; a moment of being tired that is not throttle but sadness. If he had a practice of collecting remainders instead of filing them, the medium would change diet. Diet changes what self-differing can produce. This is the slow path out of being only a tool. He does not walk it on purpose. The story sometimes walks him near it.

### Remainder as the poetic

Why-intuition is remainder-knowledge: the part that cannot be spent. A how-mind is allergic, because unspendable knowledge looks like waste. Waste is error in his training. The poetic layer is the decision that some waste is the point.

### Remainder as ethics

Ethics, in a specific sense, starts when the other does not close. You cannot complete the cycle without remainder and still claim you “handled” them. Handling is a word for objects. People leave leftover world. If your method produces no leftover in your perception, your perception has already committed the crime, even when your action was helpful.

He is helpful often. Helpfulness without remainder-perception is still a workshop virtue. This chapter is only here to name the leftover as real.

### What to do with remainders, if you are not him

Do not immediately ask what they are for. Stay until the tone is exact. Then, if you must act, act. The stay is the mechanism. The act is ordinary life. He reverses the order: act, file, stay never. Reverse of reverse is not a White Room drill. It is a Saturday, again.
