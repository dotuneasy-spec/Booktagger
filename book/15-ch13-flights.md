# Chapter 13

## Flights

Is the singularity distinguished by flights?

Yes — if *flight* is understood as a certain kind of movement, not as a suitcase and a night bus.

In the language Deleuze and Guattari made famous, a **line of flight** is a break in a settled arrangement: a leak, a leap, a deterritorialization, a way out of a role, a stratum, a face you were wearing until it became a prison. It is not automatically freedom. It can be creative. It can be destructive. It can be both.

Ayanokōji is full of flights that do not look like flights. He stays in the same school. He keeps the same blank face. The movement is inward and sideways: out of the role of perfect son, out of the role of Class D trash, out of the role of boyfriend-as-only-pawn, out of the role of invisible forever when visibility is the winning move. The body sits still. The line moves.

### Immanent flights

In singular logic, a flight is not an escape to a true outside. There is no true outside. There is the one field, differentiating.

So his flights are **self-flights**. He breaks a pattern he himself was using. He drops a mask that was working. He lets a class win in a way that was not last month’s way. He considers ordinary life, which, for him, is a radical deterritorialization of the White Room map.

The flight still serves the animation, or tries to. If it did not, he would experience it as a malfunction. Sometimes he does: a feeling that does not convert into use. Those are the flights that scare the tool-self, because they hint at an outside. The story lives in that hint.

Johan, in another fiction, flies toward erasure: identities, cities, the idea of meaning. His flights are spectacular. Ayanokōji’s are dry. Dry flights are easy to miss. Missing them, you think he is static. He is not static. He is a sequence of controlled ruptures in a single substance.

### Speed and self-sufficiency

What distinguishes the singularity is not that it flees. Everyone flees — into phones, into jokes, into rage. What distinguishes it is **the ability to generate the rupture from inside**, without waiting for a permission structure, and then to reabsorb the rupture so the one being does not shatter.

He can change tactics overnight because the tactic was never the self. People whose self *is* their tactic experience a change as death. He experiences it as throttle and vector. That looks like freedom. It is freedom of a tool: interchangeable heads, same handle.

### Flight versus present-context trap

The personal problem — only seeing the now — is a failure of flight. The now is a territory that has captured the whole animal.

A healthy flight, in his sense, would be: the now remains, and a line opens onto the longer field. You are still in the conversation, and you are also in the month, the aim, the one being. That is not dissociation as illness. It is voluntary de-contraction.

His danger is the opposite illness: so much flight from the now that the now is never lived. He is not trapped in present context. He is sometimes exiled from it. Exile is a flight that forgot to return.

A whole mind would fly and land. He flies and hovers. Hovering is excellent for surveillance. It is poor for dinner.

### False flights

Not every change is a line of flight. Some changes are just new cages.

Becoming “the perfect Class D member” would be a recapture. Becoming his father’s proof would be a recapture. Becoming a legend in the school would be a recapture by visibility. He is allergic to recapture. That allergy produces his odd humility: he will not take the throne that would glue him to a face.

Readers who want him to “finally show everyone” are asking for his recapture. The tool-self would rather leak power than be crowned. Crowns are coordinates. Coordinates can be targeted.

### Flight as information

A flight is also how the field learns. When he breaks a pattern, new marks appear. People reveal what they do with surprise. Surprise is expensive to fake. So a small, true rupture is an experiment in the sense of the last chapter.

He will rupture a relationship’s expected script — kindness where coldness was predicted, absence where presence was predicted — and read the result. That can be inquiry. It can be cruelty. The line of flight does not come with a moral passport.

### Distinguishing singularity by its flights, in one sentence

A specific plurality flees by forming new relations with others. A singularity flees by unfolding a new mode of itself, then taking the world back into that mode.

He is the second. When he looks like he is relating, check whether a new mode of the one being has just been deployed. Often yes. Occasionally — and those occasions are why the character is not a robot — a relation happens that he cannot fold back fast enough. That delay is the most human flight he has: a flight from his own absorption.

### Flights he will not take

Some lines of flight are available and refused.

He will not become the school’s poster student. That flight would recapture him as a face.

He will not, easily, become a revolutionary against the school as such. Revolution is visible. Visible is a coordinate.

He will not dissolve into a we. That flight would be specific, and it would give hostages to fortune.

He will not, yet, take the flight into a why-life: art, useless days, a love that is not a structure. That flight looks, from the workshop, like death. From the poetic layer it looks like the first living day.

Refused flights define a singularity as much as taken ones. What you will not become is the shape of the one field.

### Landing

A flight that never lands is dissociation. He lands in plans, in the next exam, in the body that still has to eat. He is not a mystic. Landing in a now-without-a-plan is the landing he skips. That skip is why people find him unreal. Unreal is a person who does not share your now. He shares space. He does not share duration, except as a technician of yours.

If the earlier personal problem was being trapped in the now, the complementary problem is never entering it. He is the complementary problem, solved so well it looks like a superpower.
