# Recapitulations

## Part I, again

The core is a singular tool-self: one field, many outputs, stolen origin, cool tone. Surface events are contractions of deeper causation. He maps instead of declaring. People have joints and small unguarded habits. What moves him is animation — self-started, self-kept, absorptive, instrumental.

If you remember only one picture from Part I: a workshop that arrived in a school and mistook the school for more workshop, while a thin wish for a park stayed in the wall.

## Part II, again

Strategy starts in his sentence, then uses their world as the only human medium a plan can run in. Problems that are already theirs produce settlements that can be said. Dialectic makes a stance thicker by including a contrary and metabolizing it. Counterpoint is two lines, still his piece. Conversation is the sensor. Perception already writes solutions: Umwelt, marks, prescriptions.

If you remember only one picture from Part II: he walks at your talking pace toward a station you did not choose, and you arrive having said a sentence that feels like yours.

## Part III, again

He learns by making do, out of necessity, not by converting to open multiplicity. He is distinguished by immanent flights that return to the one field. He differs from himself using his own medium. Intuition has a how-floor he owns and a why-floor he barely visits. Agency is ranking the now. Thinking is one being. The poetic layer is exact tone without a therefore.

If you remember only one picture from Part III: a boy who can tell more than the minute tells him, and cannot let the minute tell him enough.

## Part IV, again

Singular versus specific is the skeleton. Bergson names duration, mix, intellect, intuition. Other minds are mirrors. The father is manufacturer-weather. The school is climate. Invisibility is law. Memory presses on purpose. Freedom-from is native; freedom-for is missing. Ordinary is a flight he cannot complete. A day is ranking. Language is dry closure. Remainders will not digest. Intuitive reason is a mechanism. Winning is a metric with a hole. The image of thought is the list under the feats.

If you remember only one picture from Part IV: a specialist of unity standing in a room of people who are not folds, scoring a game that cannot grade a park.

## The bill, again

Competence is not goodness. Mapping is not meeting. Conversation is dual-use. The knife is empty. Take occupation of your own one being. Leave other people specific. Stay for a why that is not a use.

That is the book in a smaller room. The larger room was for walking until the joints could be seen without slogans, in simpler terms, with a little technicality left in so the joints would not smear.
