# Intuitive Reason

## A Mechanism, Not a Mood

The conversation’s late insight deserves a second full pass, because it is easy to hear as “be more poetic” and miss the mechanism.

### Reason that does not cut first

Ordinary reason: isolate parts, find causes, predict, control. How.

Intuitive reason: stay with a motion until its inner necessity is clear, the way a musician stays with a phrase until the next note is obvious without a theory. The obviousness is exact, not vague. Wrong notes are still wrong. The standard is qualitative fit, not a spreadsheet.

On the how-floor, he already has this for social force. A plan “clicks.” A person “will break here.” Click is not magic. It is coincidence with a pattern in the medium.

On the why-floor, click would be: this emptiness is *this* emptiness, not boredom, not nihilism-as-fashion, not rest. Exact tone. Then you know something you cannot spend. Knowing-without-spending is the mechanism. He spends. The mechanism, pointed at why, is the opposite of spending: dwelling until meaning is the motion itself.

### Why this is “almost poetic”

A poem is a machine for producing a duration of exact tone. It is not the only such machine. A look at the sea can be one. A silence after a true sentence can be one. A memory that is not used can be one. The poetic is the family name.

He produces durations in other people (a talk that lets a sentence be born). He rarely inhabits a duration that is not a means. So he is a poet of other people’s how, and a beginner at his own why. That is a strange artistic profile: influence without lyric, craft without dwelling.

### Intuitive reason and agency

If you only have cutting reason, the now is a set of parts that happen to you. If you have how-intuition, you can move in the now as a craftsman. If you have why-intuition, you can know whether the moving is a life or only a hallway.

The personal problem — seeing only present context — is helped by how-intuition (the now is a sample). It is healed, if at all, by why-intuition (this sample has a meaning that is not the next task). He offers the help. He does not offer the healing. This book tried to offer both names so you do not steal the help and call it a life.

### A last slow example

A classmate is kind to him for no contest reason.

How: what do they want, what node, what risk, what use.

How-intuition: the kindness is not a hunt; it is a habit of their bubble; low threat; file.

Why-intuition: kindness like this has a tone — unstrategic, slightly clumsy, a we trying to happen. The tone is a fact as real as a score. If you stay with it, you know what a world would have to be for this tone to be common. That knowledge is not a plan. It is a measure of how far your workshop is from a park.

He would complete the first two. This chapter is only here to insist that the third is not fluff. It is a mechanism of mind. A boy can lack it and still win a school. A reader can lack it and still copy his wins. A life that lacks it will not know what the wins were for.
