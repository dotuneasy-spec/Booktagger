# Freedom

## From, For, and the Empty Prize

Freedom is one of his few openly named aims. It needs a chapter because the word is a trap.

### Freedom-from

This is his native dialect. Freedom from the father, from being a demonstration, from a class rank that owns you, from a legend, from a feeling that dictates, from a now that fills the screen. The tool-self is an engine of negative liberty. It is very good. Many of his moves are explained by it: invisibility, refusal of thrones, leaving the Room, sampling ordinary as a not-that.

Negative liberty is real. People in cages need it. He was in a cage.

### Freedom-for

This is the missing dialect. Freedom for a day, a craft, a person, a useless hour, a we, a why. Without a for, from-freedom is a hallway that never arrives at a room. You can keep opening doors (this exam, this rival, this year) and find more hallway.

The poetic layer is the beginning of a for: this motion has a quality that is worth coinciding with, not only escaping into. He does not stay. So his prize, if he wins every from, may be a clean empty.

### Freedom and ranking

Ranking the now is freedom-from the now’s tyranny. It can kill freedom-for the now’s gift. The same act. Agency, in chapter 16, tried to keep both: optional contraction. He opted out of too many gifts.

### Freedom and other people

If others are folds, your freedom is the freedom of a monarch of a private country. If others are specific, your freedom is limited by theirs, and that limit is not only a cage. It is how a world exists. He experiences limits as constraints to route around. Sometimes they are. A father’s ownership is a cage. A friend’s claim on your evening might be a world.

He is under-practiced at telling cages from worlds. The Room taught him that claims are cages. Ordinary life is full of claims that are the substance of meaning: wait for me, stay, tell me the truth, waste this hour. He can perform them as maintenance. Performance is not consent to be limited. Consent to be limited by a specific other is a kind of freedom-for (for this bond) that looks, to the tool-self, like recapture. That misreading is tragic and consistent.

### The school’s freedom-language

The institution talks as if competition is freedom: rise, choose, earn. That language is a surface. Under it, cameras, points, and class fate. He reads the depth and uses the surface. He is not fooled by the school’s “freedom.” He may be fooled by his own: the idea that unnamed, unowned, unmatched competence is already a life. It is a hallway.

### A sentence he cannot yet say

I am free *for* this.

This would have to name a this that is not a tactic. A person, a practice, a place, a tone of days. Until he can say it without a therefore, the prize is empty and the legend will keep mistaking emptiness for cool.
