# Satellite hip hop stations

North American satellite hip hop is carried by **SiriusXM**. A subset of those channels is also on **Dish Network** audio. There is no second nationwide satellite-radio service with its own hip-hop lineup.

Channel numbers below are the standard SiriusXM satellite numbers. Your package, radio, and country decide which of them you actually receive. Stations marked explicit play frequent coarse language; Family Friendly plans clean or replace most of that programming. Lineups change. This guide matches SiriusXM’s hip-hop and R&B page and the SiriusXM channel list current as of 1 September 2026.

### Which one to put on

- **New rap, all the time:** Hip-Hop Nation (44).
- **No edits, deeper and left-of-center rap:** Shade 45 (45).
- **’80s and ’90s classics:** Rock The Bells Radio (43).
- **2000s and 2010s rap only:** Flex2K (49).
- **’90s and 2000s hip hop mixed with R&B:** SiriusXM FLY (50).

The same hip-hop page also surfaces **Off Grid Rap** (experimental rap) as app programming. It does not have a stable satellite channel number.

SiriusXM rotates limited-run channels (a 2Pac channel, a Hip-Hop Top 500 countdown, label takeovers). Those occupy a temporary slot, then leave. Check the app’s “Limited Edition” channels rather than a fixed number.

## Gone

These used to be satellite hip-hop or hip-hop-adjacent channels and are no longer on the air:

- **Sound 42** — Drake’s hip-hop channel
- **BackSpin** — old-school hip hop; the role moved to Rock The Bells Radio
- **Hot Jamz / The Eye** — the pre-merger rhythmic channel; replaced by The Heat
- **Street Beat** — dropped in 2004
- **Planet Rhyme** — international hip hop; dropped in 2004
- **Boombox** — breakbeats and old-school remixes; dropped in the 2008 Sirius/XM lineup merger
- **Wax** — hip hop and UK garage remixes; dropped in 2005

## How to listen

1. **In the car.** Tune 43, 44, 45, 49, or 50. Availability depends on the SiriusXM package in that vehicle.
2. **SiriusXM app.** Same satellite channels, plus the app-only list above. Streaming works outside satellite coverage.
3. **Dish Network.** Audio channels 99.43 through 99.48 carry Rock The Bells, Hip-Hop Nation, Shade 45, The Heat, SiriusXM FLY, and Heart & Soul. Flex2K and The Flow are not on Dish.
