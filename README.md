# Thinking as One Being

**The Mind of Ayanokoji**

A book-length literary and philosophical study of Kiyotaka Ayanokōji’s *image of thought*.

## Read this

Open **[book/FULL-MANUSCRIPT.md](book/FULL-MANUSCRIPT.md)** for the assembled markdown.

**PDF (graphic cover):** [book/Thinking-as-One-Being.pdf](book/Thinking-as-One-Being.pdf) — 6×9 in, ~368 pages, Ayanokōji cover.

Rebuild with `python3 book/build_pdf.py` (needs `pandoc` and `typst`).

Individual chapters live in `book/` if you want to read or edit them separately. Order is listed in `book/00-front-matter.md`.

## What this is

Parts I–IV follow a conversation about Ayanokōji as a magnifying glass. Parts V–IX describe singular thought as form, second, hour, diet, and the original problem of the now — then **Finis**. The book is finished.

It is **not** a reprint of *Classroom of the Elite*. It is **not** a training manual for manipulating people. A note on that sits near the front of the manuscript.

## Length

The manuscript is finished, on the order of seventy thousand words.
