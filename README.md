# Thinking as One Being

**The Mind of Ayanokoji**

A book-length literary and philosophical study of Kiyotaka Ayanokōji’s *image of thought*.

## Read this

Open **[book/FULL-MANUSCRIPT.md](book/FULL-MANUSCRIPT.md)** for the assembled book (start to finish).

Individual chapters live in `book/` if you want to read or edit them separately. Order is listed in `book/00-front-matter.md`.

## What this is

Original analysis, in mostly plain language with a little technical vocabulary (singular/specific, duration, Umwelt, intuition, self-differing, univocity, occupation, grant). Parts I–IV follow a conversation about Ayanokōji as a magnifying glass on an image of thought. **Part V** continues as singular thought itself — the form of one field, not a personality. **Part VI** continues on the second: how thought that is one can think what is not its function.

It is **not** a reprint of *Classroom of the Elite*. It is **not** a training manual for manipulating people. A note on that sits near the front of the manuscript.

## Length

The manuscript is on the order of fifty thousand words and sixty-plus sections, sized as a short trade book (around two hundred printed pages depending on type and chapter breaks).
