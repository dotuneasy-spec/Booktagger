## What this is

Parts I–IV follow a conversation about Ayanokōji as a magnifying glass. Parts V–IX describe singular thought as form, second, hour, diet, and the original problem of the now — then **Finis**. The book is finished.

It is **not** a reprint of *Classroom of the Elite*. It is **not** a training manual for manipulating people. A note on that sits near the front of the manuscript.

## Length

The manuscript is finished, on the order of seventy thousand words.
